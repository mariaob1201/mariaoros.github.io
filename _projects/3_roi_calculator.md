A decision-support calculator that estimates the economic return on investment of fungicide applications, helping growers weigh costs against expected yield protection. The tool covers **corn diseases**, **soybean white mold**, and **alfalfa foliar diseases**, and is published as part of the [Crop Protection Network](https://cropprotectionnetwork.org/roi-calculators) suite of grower-facing decision tools.

- **Live app:** [connect.doit.wisc.edu/cpn-roi-tools](https://connect.doit.wisc.edu/cpn-roi-tools/)
- **Crop Protection Network listing:** [cropprotectionnetwork.org/roi-calculators](https://cropprotectionnetwork.org/roi-calculators)
